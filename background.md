# Background

**Kaguura Gichuru**
Nairobi, Kenya

BSc Computer Science, University of Eldoret.

---

I understood two things early that have governed every decision since.

The first: time is the only resource you cannot recover. Spending it building someone else's dream at the permanent expense of your own is the one trade with no upside.

The second: wealth is not purely physical. It compounds. It attracts. It carries moral weight. Getting wealthy is not the goal — it is the prerequisite. You cannot make a meaningful impact on the world from a position of scarcity. The obligation is to get wealthy enough that the giving becomes possible at scale.

Growing up in Eldoret shaped my understanding of what inequality looks like up close — and what it demands of anyone with the capacity to do something about it. Everything I build is downstream of that.
