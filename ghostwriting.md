# Substack Ghostwriting

Kaguura Gichuru offers Substack ghostwriting for founders, executives, and operators who have important things to say but not the time or the craft to say them consistently.

## What This Is

Long-form newsletter ghostwriting. The work involves extracting your thinking, shaping it into a coherent editorial voice, and publishing consistently under your name. The goal is compounding authority — the kind that makes people trust you before they've met you.

## Social Proof

Kaguura has grown his own Substack, The Write Path, to over 2,000 subscribers in under a year, with a significantly international audience and strong American readership. The newsletter covers psychology, philosophy, and business from the perspective of a founder building in Africa.

## Who This Is For

Founders and executives with a genuine point of view who want to build an audience that outlasts any single company or role. If you're building something real, writing about it compounds in ways that paid acquisition cannot replicate.

## How It Works

Scoped per engagement. Typical structure: four long-form pieces per month, written in your voice, published on your Substack. Pricing on request.
