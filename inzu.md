# INZU

INZU is a property management SaaS built for high-net-worth landlords in Nairobi, Kenya — targeting premium residential markets including Westlands, Kilimani, and Karen.

→ [inzuprop.com](https://www.inzuprop.com)
→ Status: Approaching beta

## The Problem

Kenya's rental market is fragmented. Landlords running multiple units have no single place to see their collections, communicate with tenants, or manage repairs. Existing SaaS products don't consolidate payment modes onto one dashboard. Tenants routinely pay out of pocket for repairs that are the landlord's responsibility, or are overcharged by handymen they have no way to vet. The landlord often doesn't even know there was a problem.

## The Product

**For tenants:** WhatsApp-only interface. No app to download. Tenants raise maintenance tickets, receive updates, and communicate with management entirely through WhatsApp.

**For landlords:** Real-time dashboard showing rent collection status, unit-by-unit payment history, and maintenance activity. Rent is collected via M-Pesa Daraja API with automated PDF receipts attached to every transaction.

**For maintenance:** INZU operates a vetted handyman ecosystem. When a ticket is raised, a handyman is dispatched. No money changes hands directly between tenant and handyman — landlords are billed a fixed monthly fee per unit for maintenance coverage, regardless of repair volume. High-maintenance months are covered. Quiet months are pure margin. It runs like an insurance model. Handymen are incentivised to close tickets efficiently because their downtime scales with how well they perform.

## Monetization

- 8–10% of rent collected (primary revenue)
- Fixed monthly maintenance fee per unit
- Potential B2B channel: trust division rent collection for institutional clients

## Stack

WhatsApp Business API (tenant interface), M-Pesa Daraja API (payments), real-time landlord dashboard, AI-driven maintenance ticketing, automated PDF receipt generation.
