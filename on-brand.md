# On Brand

*Kaguura Gichuru's working philosophy on brand strategy.*

## What Brand Actually Is

Brand is not a logo. It is not a color palette. It is the set of beliefs a market holds about you before you walk into the room. The job of brand strategy is to engineer those beliefs deliberately, rather than letting them accumulate by accident.

## Aesthetics and Purpose

The two things that make work worth doing: aesthetics and purpose. Aesthetics because how something looks and feels is not superficial — it is the first signal of whether something is worth trusting. Purpose because without it, you are just moving product, and there is no version of that which is interesting to do for a long time.

## Creative Freedom

The best work requires trust. A creative partner being second-guessed at every step is not a creative partner — they are a highly paid executor. The value in working with someone who has genuine taste is that you let them use it.

## On International Recognition

Lupita Nyong'o did not build her standing by optimizing for the Kenyan market first. International recognition compounds differently — and for African builders and creatives with something genuinely original to say, the international market is the more interesting and more valuable one to reach.

## On Personal Brand

Everyone is one good idea away from a new reputation. Writing makes that possible faster than almost anything else. Consistency of published thinking — not polish, not production value, just honesty and regularity — is the simplest brand strategy available to any founder.
