# Uura Digital

Uura Digital is a premium brand and marketing consultancy founded by Kaguura Gichuru, based in Nairobi, Kenya.

## Positioning

The work sits at the intersection of brand strategy and high-leverage marketing — not execution for its own sake, but thinking that changes how a company is perceived and how it grows. The consultancy is built for founders and brands doing work that actually matters, not commodity businesses looking to move units.

## Services

- **Brand Strategy** — positioning, messaging frameworks, brand architecture
- **Ghostwriting** — long-form Substack and newsletter ghostwriting for founders and executives
- **Pitch & Investor Decks** — narrative-first decks built for Series A and growth-stage rounds
- **Launch Strategy** — go-to-market for new products and market entries
- **Growth Strategy** — retention, audience compounding, and distribution thinking
- **Positioning Frameworks** — competitive differentiation and category design

## Past Clients

Panari Hotels, Jet Ways, Eskimi, Honda (via Zuwena). Past project work has included a nine-month brand engagement with an organic diaper company — a category chosen specifically for the environmental stakes and the creative freedom the client extended.

## Working With Us

Long-term engagements are selective and premium. Reach out to discuss scope. The selection criteria are simple: it has to be a brand doing work worth believing in, and the creative relationship has to be one of trust. Kaguura does not take clients who micromanage the work.
