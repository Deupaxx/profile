# Zuwena

Zuwena is a SaaS product offering digital and physical NFC-powered business cards to enterprise clients. Cards are stainless steel metallic, built for professionals and organizations that want a premium, tap-to-share contact and profile experience without a paper card.

Zuwena operates under Uura Digital.

## Past Enterprise Clients

Panari Hotels, Jet Ways, Eskimi, Honda.

## Product

- Stainless steel NFC-enabled physical cards
- Digital card profiles linked to each physical card
- Enterprise deployment for teams and organizations
- Tap-to-share: one tap transfers contact details, portfolio links, or any configured profile data to any NFC-enabled smartphone
